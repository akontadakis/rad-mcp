# Glossary

**Analysis area** — the region over which sDA/ASE are aggregated; regularly occupied work areas,
excluding low-occupancy spaces like circulation and storage (Ch 4)

**ASE_1000,250h** — Annual Sunlight Exposure: percent of analysis area receiving ≥ 1 000 lx (*"1,000 lux or more"*, LM-83-12 §2.2.6) of
direct sunlight for > 250 h/yr, computed **before** blinds deploy, zero-bounce (Ch 4)

**Background area** — band ≥ 3 m beyond the immediate surrounding area, within the space; introduced
in EN 12464-1:2011 (Ch 1)

**BSDF** — Bidirectional Scattering Distribution Function; the angular optical description of a
complex fenestration system. Preferred over any scalar transmittance (Ch 4, Ch 5)

**Curfew** — time after which stricter obtrusive-light limits apply; often a planning condition
(Ch 2)

**D_T / D_TM** — target and minimum target **daylight factor**, EN 17037 Method 1; city-specific.
**Table A.3** (vertical/inclined openings) derives from median external *diffuse* illuminance;
**Table A.4** (horizontal openings with diffusing material) from median external *global*
illuminance. Do not read one as the other (Ch 3)

**DGP** — Daylight Glare Probability; the daylight glare metric. Not applicable to horizontal
openings (Ch 3)

**Direct sunlight (LM-83)** — ≥ 1 000 lx (*"1,000 lux or more"*, LM-83-12 §2.2.6, §3.2.2 and its Glossary) of direct beam from the solar orb, after glazing
transmittance, excluding *operable* blinds but retaining fixed shading, with **no** reflected or
diffuse component (Ch 4)

**Daylight hours (EN 17037)** — the highest **4 380** of the 8 760 rank-ordered hourly diffuse
horizontal illuminances; **not** sun-up hours. Half of them, the F_time = 50 % criterion, is
**2 190 h** (Ch 3)

**Diversity** — illuminance ratio criterion distinct from uniformity; named for railway applications
(Ch 2)

**E_m** — **maintained** illuminance. Simulation output is initial; apply the maintenance factor
before comparing (Ch 1)

**E_T / E_TM** — target and minimum target illuminance, EN 17037 (Ch 3)

**F_D** — daylight **dependency** factor, EN 15193; `F_D = 1 − (F_D,S × F_D,C)`. A simulation supplies
the *supply* factor `F_D,S` only; the control factor `F_D,C` and occupancy `F_O` are separate. Note
`F_D = 1` means **no** daylight benefit (Ch 6)

**F_DGP,exceed** — fraction of usage time the DGP threshold may be exceeded; 5 %. Reference usage
time is 08:00–18:00 Mon–Fri (Ch 3)

**F_plane,%** — fraction of the reference plane that must meet a target (Ch 3)

**F_time,%** — fraction of daylight hours a target must be met; 50 % throughout EN 17037 (Ch 3)

**g** — total solar energy transmittance; a **thermal** quantity. Not the visual one. Defined by
**EN 410**, not by EN 13363 (Ch 5)

**GR / GR_L** — CIE Glare Rating and its per-application limit; the **outdoor** discomfort glare
metric. `GR = 27 + 24·log10(L_vl / L_ve^0,9)` (Ch 2)

**Ē_z** — mean cylindrical illuminance; ≥ 50 lx, ≥ 150 lx where visual communication matters, at
1.2 m seated / 1.6 m standing. An EN 12464-1:2011 requirement (Ch 1)

**Immediate surrounding area** — band ≥ 0.5 m around the task area indoors; **≥ 2 m** outdoors
under EN 12464-2 (Ch 1, Ch 2)

**Modelling index** — `Ē_z / Ē_h` at a point, normally 1.2 m above floor; 0.30–0.60 indicates good
modelling (Ch 1)

**LENI** — Lighting Energy Numeric Indicator, kWh/m²·year, defined at **building** level (Ch 6)

**LLMF / LSF** — lamp lumen maintenance factor / lamp survival factor; components of the maintenance
factor, tabulated by lamp type and operating hours (Ch 1, Ch 6)

**Maintenance factor (MF)** — ratio of maintained to initial illuminance; must be stated with its
assumptions and schedule (Ch 1)

**Obtrusive light** — light escaping an outdoor installation into the surroundings; governed with
reference to CIE 150:2003 (Ch 2)

**Openness factor** — fraction of open area in a fabric shade; maps to **specular** transmittance in
LM-83's fallback (Ch 4)

**Perez sky** — the sky luminance distribution model LM-83 requires for hourly diffuse and direct
components (Ch 4)

**Reference plane** — the surface illuminance is assessed on; **0.85 m** in EN 17037, **0.8 m /
30 in** in LM-83 (Ch 3, Ch 4)

**Reinhart patches** — subdivision of the sky dome; LM-83 requires a minimum of **2 305** (Ch 4)

**sDA_300/50%** — Spatial Daylight Autonomy: percent of analysis area meeting 300 lx for ≥ 50 % of
the analysis period (Ch 4)

**Task area** — where the visual task is performed; may be horizontal, vertical or inclined (Ch 1)

**U_o** — illuminance uniformity, E_min / E_avg, computed **per zone**. Indoors: task 0,7 (2002) or
per-activity (2011); immediate surrounding 0,5 (2002) → **0,40** (2011); background 0,10. Outdoors:
surroundings 0,10 (Ch 1, Ch 2)

**UGR / UGR_L / UGR_R** — Unified Glare Rating; its per-task limit; and the reference-room (4H × 8H)
catalogue value that characterises a luminaire, not an installation (Ch 1)

**Upward light ratio** — fraction of luminaire flux emitted above the horizontal (Ch 2)

**View layers** — sky, landscape, ground; the content criterion for view out (Ch 3)

**Window group** — coplanar windows with similar shadow patterns and shading operation; within
**±7.5° of azimuth** may be grouped (Ch 4)

**τ_v** — light transmittance; the **visual** quantity, as opposed to `g` (Ch 5)
